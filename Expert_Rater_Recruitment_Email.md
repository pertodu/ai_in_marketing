# Expert-Rater Recruitment Emails & Incentive Guide

> **Goal:** Recruit **3–5 independent expert raters** (invite 5–6 to guarantee at least 3–4 returns) to classify the 26 detected marketing technologies in `revision_analysis\Rater_Response_Form.xlsx` using `Rater_Instructions.docx`.  
> **Independence rule:** Send each invitation **individually** (never group-CC) and state clearly that any thank-you incentive is unconditional on how they rate.

---

## Practical Incentive Menu (Choose What Fits Each Rater)

| Incentive Option | Typical Cost | Best Audience | Why It Works |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **1. \$25–\$50 e-Gift Card *or* Charity Donation** | \$25–\$50 per rater | Practitioners, PhD students, faculty | Giving a charity-donation alternative removes awkwardness for senior faculty who prefer not to take cash/cards from peers. |
| **2. Reciprocal Academic Favor ("I owe you one")** | \$0 | Marketing / IS faculty peers | Offering a guaranteed 15–20 min pretest, scale rating, or friendly paper read in return gets very high response rates among faculty. |
| **3. Exclusive 1-Page Benchmark Brief (1,000 US Firms)** | \$0 | Martech practitioners, CMOs, consultants | High perceived value: practitioners rarely see size-balanced martech adoption rates (<\$10M to >\$1B) paired with Google rating/review benchmarks. |
| **4. Optional Named Acknowledgment in the Manuscript** | \$0 | Faculty, PhD students, consultants | Signals scholarly respect; always make it *optional* so raters can stay anonymous if preferred. |
| **5. Formal Thank-You / Service Verification Note** | \$0 | PhD students, postdocs, adjuncts | Useful documentation for academic service portfolios or industry advisory profiles. |

> **Ethics note:** Never tie incentives to agreement or specific answers, and do not offer co-authorship for a 15–20 minute classification task (per COPE authorship standards).
